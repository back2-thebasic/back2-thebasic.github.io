I am particularly interested in using human demonstrations and egocentric data to help robots acquire manipulation skills and interact with the physical world. I am also interested in dexterous manipulation and whole-body loco-manipulation.

Safe Dexterous Pen Spinning with the ORCA Hand

Peiqi Liu, Linxuan Chen, Cheng Su, Jiazhen Niu, Autumn Kwon

CSCI 513 Course Project · Fall 2026 · Ongoing

We investigate in-hand pen spinning with the ORCA dexterous hand. Building on human hand-motion retargeting and preliminary pen-reorientation results in simulation, we explore reinforcement learning for coordinated finger control. Our goal is to develop reliable pen-spinning skills while incorporating safety-aware motion constraints.
