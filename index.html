Cheng Su

I am Cheng Su, a first-year M.S. student in Computer Science at the University of Southern California (USC). My research interests lie in egocentric data, robot learning, and robot manipulation. I am particularly interested in using human demonstrations and egocentric data to help robots acquire manipulation skills and interact with the physical world. I am also interested in dexterous manipulation and whole-body loco-manipulation.
